About IRANMEX

IRANMEX, the country’s largest specialized event for the mountaineering, climbing and outdoor recreation industry, takes place on 9 and 10 December 2026, from 12:00 to 22:00, at Tehran’s Parsian Enghelab Hotel. Coinciding with the observance of International Mountain Day and the seventh Mountain Writings Festival, the exhibition welcomes Iran’s mountaineering and outdoor community.

IRANMEX is owned by Aso Puyesh Datis (BERC) and held in official partnership with the Alpine Club of Iran and the Mountaineering and Sport Climbing Federation of Iran. Leading companies, manufacturers, suppliers and service providers in mountaineering, climbing, outdoor equipment and related industries present their latest achievements and innovations.

Exhibition themes

  1. Technical equipment and specialist apparel

  2. Nutrition, sports supplements and energy management at altitude

  3. Technology, biometric monitoring and digital solutions

  4. Clubs, training and international qualifications

  5. Health, relief, rescue and mountain medicine

  6. Adventure tourism, ecotourism and expeditions

  7. Media, documentary, literature and mountain culture

  8. Environmental protection, ecology and green events

IRANMEX

Why was IRANMEX created?

Iran lacked a comprehensive, specialized and standardized exhibition and infrastructure for its mountaineering and outdoor industry. IRANMEX was created to meet that need and bring together professionals, manufacturers and the mountain community.

The event aims to improve the quality and safety of domestically produced equipment and create a national platform for presenting the industry’s capabilities and achievements to international markets.
