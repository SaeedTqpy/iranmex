About the event

About IRANMEX

The national specialized exhibition for mountaineering, climbing, outdoor recreation, equipment and related industries

IRANMEX
EXPO

A national gathering of the mountain community

The national specialized exhibition for mountaineering, climbing, outdoor recreation, equipment and related industries brings together professionals, manufacturers, pioneers and enthusiasts of mountains and nature.

The event aims to encourage collaboration, present the latest industrial and scientific achievements, and improve safety and standards in the field.

Dates
9–10 December 2026
Visiting hours
12:00–22:00
Venue
Parsian Enghelab Hotel, Taleghani Street, Tehran

Organizer and strategic partners

The exhibition is planned and delivered by Aso Puyesh Datis, owner of the IRANMEX brand, in official partnership with the Alpine Club of Iran and the Mountaineering and Sport Climbing Federation of Iran. It aims to provide the audience with the highest professional and operational standards.

Vision, mission and social responsibility

Vision

To become the most trusted national and regional platform for advancing outdoor and nature tourism industries and services.

Mission

To strengthen collaboration across the mountaineering ecosystem, improve the quality and safety of domestically produced equipment, and present national capabilities to global markets.

Social responsibility

Monitor and calculate the exhibition’s carbon footprint and plant trees to help protect valuable mountain ecosystems.
